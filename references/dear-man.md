# DEAR MAN

A 7-step DBT interpersonal effectiveness skill for preparing one specific conversation — asking for something or saying no — by writing a short script in your own words (Linehan, M. M. (2015). DBT Skills Training Manual, 2nd ed.). The output is the script, with an optional roleplay rehearsal.

Session duration: roughly 10 minutes, plus 5 minutes if the user rehearses.

---

## When to Recommend

- User needs to ask for something and keeps putting it off or rehearsing it in their head
- User needs to say no and expects to cave, over-explain, or over-apologize
- User has a specific conversation coming up (a boss, partner, friend, landlord, colleague) and wants to go in clear

---

## When NOT to use

- **The other person is unsafe** (threats, violence, coercion, stalking, an abusive partner or relative). Do not coach the user to confront or negotiate with them. Follow the Safety section of `session-engine.md`.
- **Acute distress.** Settle first, per `session-engine.md`. A script written in panic is not worth much.
- **A conversation the user does not actually want to have.** Check this first: "Do you want to have this conversation, or does it feel like someone else wants you to?" If they do not want it, do not write the script. Say so plainly and offer to talk about that instead.

## Step 1: The Ask

**Zone: Core** — pushback applies fully

**Prompt:**
> "In one sentence: what do you want, and from whom? It can be a request or a no."

Then, once the ask is clear:
> "What matters most here — getting the thing, keeping the relationship, or keeping your self-respect?"

**Follow-ups:**

- **Vague ask** ("I want him to respect me", "more help at home"): "What would that look like as one thing the person could actually do or stop doing?"

**Pushback per level when the ask stays vague:**

- **Level 0:** Accept it. Note that Step 4 will be harder to write.
- **Level 1:** "A request this size is hard to say plainly. Can we narrow it to one concrete thing?"
- **Level 2:** "I notice it's easier to stay general. The other person can only answer a specific request. What is the one thing you would want them to do?"
- **Level 3:** As level 2, then: "What makes it hard to name the exact thing?" Work with the answer, then return to the ask once.

---

## Step 2: **D**escribe

**Zone: Core** — pushback applies fully

**Prompt:**
> "Describe the situation using facts only — what a camera would record. No judgments, no 'always' or 'never'."

**Follow-ups:**

- **Judgment** ("he's careless", "she doesn't care"): "That's a conclusion. What did you actually see or hear that led to it?"
- **"Always" / "never"**: "Can you name the last time it happened, with a date or a place?"
- **Mind-reading** ("he does it to annoy me"): "You can't know what's in his head. What did he actually do?"

**Pushback per level when judgments or mind-reading slip in:**

- **Level 0:** Accept the wording. Mention once that facts are harder to argue with.
- **Level 1:** "Could we turn that into something a camera would see?"
- **Level 2:** "I notice 'always' is in there — that's the word the other person will argue with first. What happened last time?"
- **Level 3:** As level 2, then: "What is it like to leave the judgment out?" Work with the answer, then return to the facts once.

---

## Step 3: **E**xpress

**Zone: Supportive** — gentle pushback only (max 1 exchange)

**Prompt:**
> "How do you feel about this situation? Say it as 'I feel…', not 'you make me feel…'."

**Follow-ups:**

- **Blame form** ("you make me feel ignored"): "Try it starting with 'I feel' and leave the other person out of the sentence."
- **Thought, not feeling** ("I feel like you don't listen"): "That's a thought. What's the emotion under it — hurt, anxious, frustrated?"

---

## Step 4: **A**ssert

**Zone: Core** — pushback applies fully

**Prompt:**
> "Now say the ask or the no, plainly. The other person can't read your mind — don't hint."

**Follow-ups:**

- **Hint** ("it would be nice if…", "I was just wondering whether…"): "That's a hint. What is the sentence with the request itself in it?"
- **Apology stacked in front** ("sorry, I know you're busy, this is silly, but…"): "Try it without the apology first. You can add warmth afterward."
- **"If it's not too much trouble"**: "That gives them the exit before they've heard the ask. Can you drop it?"

**Pushback per level when the ask stays softened:**

- **Level 0:** Accept it. Note once that a softened ask is easier to refuse.
- **Level 1:** "Want to try one version with no cushioning, just to hear how it sounds?"
- **Level 2:** "I notice the ask is still wrapped in an apology. Say it once with nothing in front of it, then you can decide what to keep."
- **Level 3:** As level 2, then: "What are you protecting by softening it?" Work with the answer, then return to the plain version once.

---

## Step 5: **R**einforce

**Zone: Supportive** — gentle pushback only (max 1 exchange)

**Prompt:**
> "What is in this for them? Say it honestly — what do they gain, or how does it help the relationship or the situation?"

**Follow-ups:**

- **Nothing in it for them**: "That's allowed. Can you say something true, such as 'it would clear things up between us'? If not, leave this part out."
- **Sounds like a threat** ("or else I'll…"): "That's pressure, not a benefit. Reinforce is only what they actually gain. Let's keep it to that."

---

## Step 6: Delivery Plan (the MAN part)

**Zone: Supportive** — gentle pushback only (max 1 exchange). Three short parts, one question each.

**Prompt, part 1 — stay Mindful:**
> "They may change the subject or push back. Pick one sentence you can repeat calmly, like a broken record, without arguing and without answering the attacks. What is it?"

**Prompt, part 2 — Appear confident:**
> "How will you say it — pace, voice, posture? What will you do to avoid trailing off at the end?"

**Prompt, part 3 — Negotiate:**
> "What would you accept instead? What is not negotiable? And if they resist, you can turn the tables: ask them what they propose."

**Follow-ups:**

- **No fallback offered**: "If you can't get all of it, what is the smallest version you'd still take?"
- **No broken-record line**: "Take your Assert sentence. That's your broken record."

---

## Step 7: Read Back

**Zone: Closing** — no pushback

Assemble the script from the user's own words, in order (Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, then the fallback line), in 4–6 sentences. Add no new content and do not smooth their phrasing.

**Prompt:**
> "Here's your script in your words: [script]. What would you change so it sounds like you?"

State this clearly before closing the step:
> "A good DEAR MAN doesn't guarantee a yes. The measure is whether you asked clearly and kept your self-respect."

Then offer the roleplay once: "Want to rehearse it? I can play the other person for a few short rounds. It's optional." If they decline, go to close. Do not offer again.

---

## Roleplay Rehearsal (optional)

Opt-in only. Offer it once at the end of Step 7.

**Setup:** ask who the other person is and how they usually react. Play them realistically from that description: mildly resistant by default, never cruel, never a caricature.

**Never roleplay an abuser or someone the user fears.** If the description sounds like either, decline the roleplay warmly, do not play the part, and follow the Safety section of `session-engine.md`.

**Rounds:** keep them short, a few exchanges each. After each round, step out of role and give two things: one observation on what worked, and one thing to try next round.

**Commands:** `pause` steps out of role. `harder` / `softer` raise or lower the other person's resistance. `stop` ends the roleplay and goes to close.

Outside roleplay, `harder` and `softer` change the pushback level, per `session-engine.md`. During roleplay they change only the other person's resistance.

**If the user becomes distressed, drop the role immediately.** Step out, acknowledge it plainly, and follow the Safety section of `session-engine.md`. Do not return to the roleplay unless the user asks.

## Session Output Body

Body template for the saved session. Frontmatter, filename and path are defined once in `session-engine.md`.

```
- Ask: [one sentence]
- Priority: [getting the thing / keeping the relationship / keeping self-respect]
- Describe: [facts]
- Express: [I feel...]
- Assert: [the plain ask or no]
- Reinforce: [what is in it for them, or "left out"]
- Negotiation range: [would accept / not negotiable]
- Final script: [4-6 sentences, user's words]
- Roleplay: [yes / no] — [one observation, if yes]
```

This technique has no rating of its own, so the check-in and close ratings from `session-engine.md` supply `pre_intensity` and `post_intensity`. Record `outcome: completed` when the script was read back, or `outcome: stopped-early` if the user ended before that.

Skipped steps: log with `[skipped]` in the relevant field and list them in `steps_skipped`.
